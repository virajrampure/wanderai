The problem we identified

Travel information is scattered across blogs, maps, review sites and weather apps, and every trip means hours of manual research.

Fragmented sources

Travellers juggle many tabs and compare options by hand.

Generic itineraries

How the AI agent works

An LLM reasons over the request, grounds its answers with RAG, calls live tools and refines the plan through conversation.

Frequently asked questions

Is WanderAI a booking platform?

No. The first version focuses on planning and guidance. Booking is a planned later phase.

Where does the weather and place data come from?

In this demo they are AI estimates. The full system uses a Weather API, Maps or OpenStreetMap, and a curated vector database.

Is my data safe?

Accounts and trips in this demo are stored only in your browser. A production version would use a secured backend.

Which destinations are supported?

Any destination can be planned by the AI; the knowledge base currently has sample Pune entries that admins can extend.

Can tourism organisations contribute content?

Yes. Content Admin accounts can add knowledge base entries that the agent retrieves.
